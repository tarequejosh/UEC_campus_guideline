Preparedness

Earthquake Safety

Earthquakes are common in Japan. Know what to do before, during, and after.

When the ground starts shaking:

1 Drop

Get low to the ground.

2 Cover

Protect your head and neck.

3 Hold On

Stay under a strong desk.

What to do during an earthquake
  • Stay away from windows, shelves, lights, and furniture that may fall.
  • Do not rush outside while the ground is shaking.
  • Do not try to turn off fire during strong shaking.
  • If possible, open the door to secure an exit.
After the shaking stops
  • Check for injuries, fires, gas smells, and building damage.
  • Get information from UEC, Chofu City, television, or your phone.
  • Do not use elevators. Use the stairs when evacuating.
  • If the building is safe, remain there and follow instructions from UEC or dormitory staff.
Emergency phone alerts

Earthquake Early Warning

Strong shaking may arrive within only a few seconds. Immediately protect yourself instead of reading all the details.

Level 3

People who need more time should begin evacuating.

Level 4

Everyone in a dangerous area should evacuate.

Level 5

Take immediate action to protect your life.

Do not wait for Alert Level 5. Evacuate by Alert Level 4.

Typhoon Safety

Typhoon season runs June–October. Stay indoors during storms.

🌪️

Before a Typhoon

  • Stock water and non-perishable food for 3 days
  • Charge all devices and power banks
  • Bring in outdoor furniture and plants
  • Check evacuation zone map
⚠️

During a Typhoon

  • Stay indoors and away from windows
  • Follow NHK World or Y! Disaster app for updates
  • Do not go outside unless absolutely necessary
  • If flooding occurs, move to higher floor
✅

Emergency Kit

  • Water (3L per person per day)
  • Non-perishable food + can opener
  • Flashlight + spare batteries
  • First aid kit + prescription meds
  • Portable charger for phone
  • Whistle (to signal for help)

🚫

Lost or Stolen Items

  • Lost passport: Go to your embassy immediately. They will issue an emergency travel document.
  • Lost wallet: Cancel cards. Report to police (110). Check at koban (police box).
  • Lost IC card: Report at station ticket office. Unused balance can be transferred.
  • Lost phone: Contact your mobile provider to suspend the line.
