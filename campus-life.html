Eating Well

Food & Dining

Navigating the UEC Cafeteria and finding the right ingredients in Chofu.

🍱

UEC Cafeteria

The 100-Yen Breakfast: Arrive early (8:00–8:45 AM) for the famous UEC breakfast set. Highly competitive but saves money!

Dietary Labels: Color-coded labels indicate allergens. Use Google Translate on ingredients lists — many Japanese broths contain pork extract or mirin.

Location: Building 1 · Mon–Fri 8:00–18:00

🛒

Halal, Vegan & Global Spices

  • Halal Gyomu Super: Frozen Halal chicken, imported spices, cheap bulk.
  • Vegetarian Kaldi (PARCO): International ingredients, lentils, imported pasta.
  • General Trianglo & Seiyu: Seiyu open 24h for late-night study sessions.

Facilities

Campus Facilities

🏫

Library

The UEC Library has study spaces, computers, and a large collection of books and journals. Open until 21:00 on weekdays. International students can borrow up to 10 items.

💻

Computer Labs

Open-access computer labs are available in East-1 and East-6 buildings. Printers are available with your student ID card. WiFi covers the entire campus.

🏃

Sports & Gym

UEC has a gymnasium, tennis courts, and a track field. Join a sports club to access facilities. The gym is free for students. Equipment can be borrowed at the counter.

Mental Health Support

UEC offers free, confidential counseling for international students. Sessions are available in English and Japanese.

  • UEC Counseling Room: Building 1, 2nd floor
  • 24-hour Hotline: 0120-07-7000 (English available)
  • Tokyo English Life Line: 03-5774-0992
Community

Clubs, Events & Culture

Clubs, circles, festivals, and events at UEC throughout the year.

🎪

Student Clubs

UEC has 50+ student clubs — sports, music, tech, culture, and more. Join during the shinkan (new member) period in April and October. International students are welcome.

🎊

Festivals & Events

UEC Festival (November): The biggest campus event with food stalls, performances, and exhibitions. Chofu Summer Festival (August): Fireworks near the Tama River.

⛩

Study Spaces

Beyond the library, try: Building 1 lobby (open late), East-6 student lounge, nearby coffee shops (Tully's at Chofu Station), or the PARCO food court (free WiFi).

Future

Career & Language

Preparing for your future career in Japan or abroad.

💼

Part-Time Jobs (Arubaito)

International students can work up to 28 hours/week (48 hours during breaks). Get a work permit from the immigration office. Common jobs: convenience store, restaurant, tutoring English.

🎓

Career Center

UEC's Career Center offers resume reviews, interview practice, and job listings. They host career fairs twice a year. Many tech companies recruit directly from UEC.

🌍

Japanese Language

UEC offers Japanese language courses for international students (free). Aim for JLPT N2 or N1 for job hunting in Japan. Use apps like Anki, WaniKani, and Bunpro for self-study.
