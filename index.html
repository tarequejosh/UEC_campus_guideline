Getting Started

Day One Essentials

Your first 24 hours in Chofu — everything you need to get connected and settled.

01
📶

On-Campus Wi-Fi

UEC-Wireless

Access the UEC network with your student ID (e.g., x2410…). Visit the IT Center on the 4th floor of East-1 if you have password issues.

02
🏢

Address Registration

Chofu City Hall

Within 14 days of moving in, take your Residence Card and Passport to Chofu City Hall. It’s a 5-minute walk south of Chofu Station.

03
🏥

Health Insurance & Pension

Mandatory

While at City Hall, sign up for National Health Insurance and apply for the Student National Pension Exemption to waive payments while studying.

04
🏦

JP Bank Account

Finance

Open a Japan Post Bank account — the most international-student-friendly option. Bring your stamped Residence Card and registered phone number.

UEC Cafeteria Secrets

The 100-Yen Breakfast: Arrive early (8:00–8:45 AM) for the famous UEC breakfast set. Highly competitive but saves money!


Dietary Labels: Color-coded labels indicate allergens. Use Google Translate on ingredients lists — many Japanese broths contain pork extract or mirin.

Halal, Vegan & Global Spices

  • Halal/Spices Gyomu Super: Frozen Halal chicken, imported spices, cheap bulk.
  • Vegetarian Kaldi (PARCO): International ingredients, lentils, imported pasta.

Local Living Hacks

Mastering the Chofu lifestyle on a student budget.

♻️

Garbage Disposal

Chofu requires designated bags. Red = burnable, Blue = non-burnable, Transparent = recyclable plastics. Never mix them!

🪙

100-Yen Shops

Daiso in PARCO and Seria nearby. Buy kitchenware, cleaning supplies, and stationery for cheap.

🚲

Secondhand Goods

Hard-Off/Off-House or the Jimoty app. Check UEC message boards for graduating seniors selling items cheaply.

Disaster Preparedness

Earthquake Safety

When the ground starts shaking:

1 Drop

Get low to the ground.

2 Cover

Protect your head and neck.

3 Hold On

Stay under a strong desk.

What to do during an earthquake
  • Stay away from windows, shelves, lights, and furniture that may fall.
  • Do not rush outside while the ground is shaking.
  • Do not try to turn off fire during strong shaking.
  • If possible, open the door to secure an exit.
After the shaking stops
  • Check for injuries, fires, gas smells, and building damage.
  • Get information from UEC, Chofu City, television, or your phone.
  • Do not use elevators. Use the stairs when evacuating.
  • If the building is safe, remain there and follow instructions from UEC or dormitory staff.
Evacuate if the building is seriously damaged, a fire has started, you smell gas, or an evacuation order has been issued.
Emergency phone alerts

Earthquake Early Warning

Strong shaking may arrive within only a few seconds. Immediately protect yourself instead of reading all the details.

Level 3

People who need more time should begin evacuating.

Level 4

Everyone in a dangerous area should evacuate.

Level 5

Take immediate action to protect your life.

Do not wait for Alert Level 5. Evacuate by Alert Level 4.
