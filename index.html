Day One Essentials

Your first 24 hours in Chofu. Follow these steps to get connected and registered.

1
📶

On-Campus Wi-Fi

UEC-Wireless

Access the UEC network by logging in with your student ID (e.g., x2410...). Visit the Information Technology Center (ITC) on the 4th floor of the East-1 building if you have password issues.

2
🏢

Address Registration

Chofu City Hall

Within 14 days of moving in, take your Residence Card and Passport to Chofu City Hall to register your address. It's a 5-minute walk south of Chofu Station.

3
🏥

Health Insurance & Pension

Mandatory

While at City Hall, sign up for National Health Insurance and apply for the Student National Pension Exemption to waive pension payments while studying.

4
🏦

Japan Post (JP) Bank

Finance

Open a JP Bank account — the most international-student-friendly bank in Japan. Bring your stamped Residence Card and registered phone number. Branch near UEC.

Food & Dietary Survival

Navigating the UEC Cafeteria and sourcing ingredients around Chofu.

🍱

UEC Cafeteria Secrets

The 100-Yen Breakfast: Arrive early (8:00-8:45 AM) for the famous UEC breakfast set. Highly competitive but saves money!


Dietary Labels: Color-coded labels indicate allergens. Use Google Translate on ingredients lists — many Japanese broths contain pork extract or mirin.

🛒

Halal, Vegan & Global Spices

  • Halal/Spices Gyomu Super: Frozen Halal chicken, imported spices, cheap bulk.
  • Vegetarian Kaldi (PARCO): International ingredients, lentils, imported pasta.
  • General Trianglo & Seiyu: Seiyu open 24h for late-night study sessions.

Local Living Hacks

Mastering the Chofu lifestyle on a student budget.

♻️

Garbage Disposal

Chofu requires designated bags. Red = burnable, Blue = non-burnable, Transparent = recyclable plastics. Never mix them!

🪙

100-Yen Shops

Daiso in PARCO and Seria nearby. Buy kitchenware, cleaning supplies, and stationery for cheap.

🚲

Secondhand Goods

Hard-Off/Off-House or the Jimoty app. Check UEC message boards for graduating seniors selling items cheaply.

Emergency & Medical

Critical information to keep you safe and healthy.

🚨

English-Speaking Clinics

Use the Tokyo Medical Institution Information (Himawari) service to find clinics in Chofu that speak English.


For minor issues, the UEC Health Care Center on campus provides free consultations.

🌍

Disaster Preparedness

  • Earthquakes: Drop, Cover, Hold on. Don't rush outside.
  • Alerts: J-Alert on your phone. Stay calm.
  • Evacuation: UEC campus is a designated evacuation site.
