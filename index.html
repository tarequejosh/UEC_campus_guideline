Arrival Welcome to Japan!

Visa & COE

Your university will send a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) to your home address. Take it to the nearest Japanese embassy to apply for your student visa. Start 3 months before departure.

Flight & Airport

Book flights to Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND). Haneda is closer to Chofu (30 min by car, 50 min by train). Narita is farther (2 hours) but has more international flights.

Airport → Chofu

From Haneda: Take Keikyu Line to Shinagawa, then Keio Line to Chofu (50 min, ~800 yen). Or take the Airport Limousine Bus to Chofu Station.

From Narita: Take Narita Express to Shinjuku, then Keio Line to Chofu (2 hours, ~3,500 yen).

Packing Checklist

Pack light — you can buy almost everything in Japan. Focus on documents, electronics, and items that are hard to find or expensive in Japan.

SIM Card & Pocket WiFi

Get a prepaid SIM or pocket WiFi before you arrive so you have internet immediately. Major providers: Mobal, Sakura Mobile, Japan Wireless.

You can also get a Japanese SIM at Bic Camera or Yodobashi Camera near Chofu once you have your residence card.

Accommodation

UEC offers International House (IH) dormitories for new international students. Apply early as spaces are limited. Private apartments near Chofu Station cost 60,000–100,000 yen/month.

Essential Apps

Install these before you arrive:

  • Google Maps — navigation & train times
  • Google Translate — camera translation for menus & signs
  • LINE — messaging (everyone in Japan uses this)
  • Japan Transit Planner — train routes
  • NHK World — English news & emergency alerts
  • PayPay — mobile payments (get after arrival)
Getting Started

Your First Week

Step-by-step guide to your first 7 days at UEC — check off each task as you complete it.

01

On-Campus Wi-Fi

UEC-Wireless

Access the UEC network using your student ID (e.g., x2410…). Visit the IT Center on the 4th floor of East-1 if you have password issues.

02

Address Registration

Chofu City Hall

Within 14 days of moving in, take your Residence Card and Passport to Chofu City Hall. It's a 5-minute walk south of Chofu Station.

03

Health Insurance & Pension

Mandatory

While at City Hall, sign up for National Health Insurance. Also apply for the Student National Pension Exemption to waive payments while studying.

04

JP Bank Account

Finance

Open a Japan Post Bank account — the most international-student-friendly option. Bring your Residence Card, registered phone number, and Hanko stamp.

05

My Number

Identification

My Number is a 12-digit personal ID for taxes, healthcare, and social services. Notification will arrive by post at your registered address a few weeks after registration.

06

IC Card

Transport

Get a PASMO or SUICA IC card at any train station ticket machine. Use it for trains, buses, convenience stores, and vending machines. Refundable deposit of 500 yen.

07

Visit International Student Office

Important

Visit the International Student Office at UEC to introduce yourself, confirm your registration, ask about orientation events, and get information about support services.

08

Get a Japanese Phone Number

Communication

Visit a mobile provider near Chofu Station. Major options: Rakuten Mobile (cheap, English app), IIJmio (budget), LINE MOBILE, or major carriers (docomo/au/SoftBank).
